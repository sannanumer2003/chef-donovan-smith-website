Eleven Years Of Cooking
Wherever The Table Gets Set.

Beachfront villas. Private yachts. Gardens lit by nothing but string lights. Wherever the table gets set, in The Bahamas or anywhere a client flies him to, the food and the service hold the same standard.

Who We Are

He's cooked dinner for two on a private beach and dinner for forty at a corporate retreat, sometimes in the same month. Villas, yachts, gardens, hotel suites, it doesn't change how he runs the kitchen. The same kitchen discipline has traveled with him to Vancouver and Toronto, for clients across the Americas who wanted that same table somewhere else.

He doesn't work alone. The chefs Donovan brings onto a job ran professional kitchens long before they ever worked one of his. He hires people who can hold a service under pressure, not just plate something that photographs well.

Today, that standard operates as Chef Donovan Smith. Donovan remains the founder and creative director. Every menu starts with him, but the team he built is the one who delivers it, whether he's on-site or not.

Every menu gets built around the people eating it, not the other way round. Get the food right, get the room right, and get out of the way so the night can be the one people bring up for years.
