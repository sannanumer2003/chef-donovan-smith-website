For more than 11 years, Chef Donovan Smith and his team have been crafting bespoke culinary experiences designed to inspire connection, celebrate life’s finest moments, and create lasting memories across The Bahamas and beyond.

Who We Are

From intimate beachfront dinners and private villa experiences to large-scale luxury events, we have proudly delivered premium dining services throughout the entire Bahamas — working with high-net-worth clientele, luxury properties, private yachts, and exclusive gatherings.

Our reputation has expanded internationally, with culinary experiences curated in Vancouver, British Columbia, Toronto, and across the Americas. Wherever the table is set, the standard remains the same.

Led by Chef Donovan, our team is comprised of some of the finest culinary professionals in the industry — chefs who bring world-class skill, innovation, and hospitality to every table. We believe dining is more than food; it is an experience that should engage the senses, tell a story, and leave a lasting impression.

Every menu is thoughtfully customised, every detail carefully executed, and every guest treated to the highest level of service and sophistication. At the heart of our brand is one mission: creating extraordinary dining experiences that inspire, connect, and elevate every occasion.
