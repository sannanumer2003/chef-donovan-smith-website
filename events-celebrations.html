Milestone Occasions

Events & Celebrations

Overview

One Standard, Any Guest Count

Whether it is a private birthday dinner, milestone celebration or corporate gathering, every event is planned around the same principles: thoughtful menus, disciplined execution and attentive service.

What's Included
  • Event catering across any guest count
  • Full table service or live station format
  • Custom menu design built around the occasion
  • Bar and beverage service
  • Complete setup, full service, and cleanup
How it Works
01

Share the brief

The occasion, date, guest count, venue, and the tone you are going for.

02

Initial call

A short call to understand the occasion more fully and discuss the format, venue, and guest profile.

03

Menu proposal and pricing

Following the consultation, we will prepare a tailored menu and pricing proposal for your review. Adjust anything before sign-off.

04

Chef and full team on the day

Your culinary and service team arrives ahead of service to complete setup, preparation and final coordination, then manages the experience through the final course.

Planning an Event?

Share the occasion, date and guest count. Our team will prepare a menu proposal and a quote.
