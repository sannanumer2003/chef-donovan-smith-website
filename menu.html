Starters

Conch Fritters

Bahamian-style conch fritters seasoned with scotch bonnet, bell pepper and fresh herbs, served with a zesty citrus aioli.

Oxtail Egg Rolls

Slow-braised Caribbean oxtail wrapped in a crispy shell with caramelised onion and thyme, served with a tamarind dipping sauce.

Crab & Plantain Bites

Sweet plantain cups filled with fresh blue crab, mango salsa and a touch of habanero — a signature Chef Donovan creation.

Lobster Bisque

Bahamian spiny lobster bisque, slow-cooked with coconut cream, saffron and a hint of dark rum. Rich, velvety, unforgettable.

Mains

Plantain Crust Jerk Chicken

A Chef Donovan signature — free-range chicken marinated in authentic jerk spices, encrusted in sweet plantain and finished on the grill.

Grilled Bahamian Lobster

Whole spiny lobster, split and grilled over charcoal, basted with garlic butter, scotch bonnet and fresh thyme. Simply extraordinary.

Caribbean Snapper

Whole red snapper steamed in banana leaf with coconut milk, ginger, scotch bonnet and island spices. Served with festival and rice peas.

Braised Short Rib

48-hour braised prime short rib with a dark rum and guava glaze, served over truffle breadfruit mash with crispy shallots.

Coconut Curry Shrimp

Tiger prawns in a slow-cooked coconut and scotch bonnet curry with turmeric rice, toasted coconut and fresh mango chutney.

Bespoke Chef’s Selection

A fully custom course crafted around your preferences, dietary needs and the finest ingredients available that day. Available on all menus.

Desserts

Rum Cake & Coconut Ice Cream

A warm Bahamian rum cake soaked in dark rum syrup, served alongside house-churned toasted coconut ice cream and caramelised pineapple.

Mango Panna Cotta

Silky coconut panna cotta layered with a fresh mango and passion fruit coulis, topped with crispy tapioca pearls and edible flowers.

Chocolate & Chilli Fondant

Dark Trinidadian chocolate fondant with a molten centre of scotch bonnet ganache, served with vanilla bean cream and gold leaf.

Seasonal Sorbet Selection

Three hand-crafted sorbets made from the finest seasonal island fruits — soursop, guava and tamarind. Light, refreshing and pure.
