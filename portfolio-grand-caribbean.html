About This Experience

Fifty Tables,
One Vision

The brief was simple and ambitious in equal measure: create a celebration dinner for over fifty guests on Nassau that would feel as personal as a dinner for four. The client had grown up in the Bahamas, built a business that spanned the Caribbean, and wanted to mark a significant milestone with an evening that honoured both where he came from and where he had arrived.

Chef Donovan's response was a grand Modern Caribbean feast — a menu of twelve dishes served across five courses, each one drawing on a different island's culinary tradition. Jamaican jerk preparations sat alongside Barbadian flying fish. Trinidad-inspired spice oils were pressed at the table. A whole-roasted pig, marinated for 36 hours in a blend of island herbs, anchored the centrepiece of the meal.

The evening ran across the grounds of a private estate, with long communal tables beneath the palms, lanterns strung between the trees, and live music building in intensity as the night deepened. By the time the final dessert was served — a rum-soaked rum cake with coconut sorbet, the client's grandmother's recipe adapted by Chef Donovan — the entire room was on its feet.
