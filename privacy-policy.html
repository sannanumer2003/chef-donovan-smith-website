Privacy
Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry or reservation request on this website, we collect the personal information you provide — which may include your name, email address, phone number, event date, event type, guest count, and any dietary or allergy information you choose to share. We do not collect payment card details directly through this website.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to respond to your inquiry, manage your booking, send confirmations and updates, and prepare for your event — for example, planning menus around dietary needs you have disclosed. We do not use your personal information for marketing or promotional purposes without your prior consent.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only with members of Chef Donovan’s team who are directly involved in your event, or where disclosure is required by law.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us, and we will action that request within a reasonable timeframe.

Cookies

This website may use basic cookies to support site functionality. We do not use third-party advertising or behavioural tracking cookies.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at booking@chefdonsmith.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of this website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please reach out at booking@chefdonsmith.com.
