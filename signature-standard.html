Preparation

How we prepare for every engagement

Every booking starts the same way: a conversation about who's eating, what they love, and what the room needs to feel like. From there, Chef Don builds the menu himself and tastes every dish before it's finalized. Nothing goes on a table he hasn't approved.

Ingredients are sourced as close to the day of service as the location allows, and the full plan, from courses to timing, is shared with you before anyone starts cooking.

The team

The team behind the table

Chef Don doesn't send whoever's available. Every chef and server on a booking has run professional service before working his: people who can hold a room under pressure, not just plate something that photographs well.

For larger events, the same core team leads every booking, so the standard doesn't reset from one job to the next.

On location

On location, any location

Villa kitchen, yacht galley, or a beach with nothing but a folding table: the team adapts the setup, not the standard.

Equipment, staffing, and timing are planned around whatever kitchen actually exists at the location, confirmed before the day of service so there are no surprises when the team arrives.

The guest experience

What guests experience from start to finish

From the moment the team arrives to the last plate cleared, service runs quietly in the background. Dietary needs and preferences are confirmed in advance so nothing has to be asked at the table.

The goal isn't to be noticed: it's for the evening to feel effortless for everyone in the room except the people doing the work.
